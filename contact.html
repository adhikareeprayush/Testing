Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our organic products, delivery, and services. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly!

All our products are certified organic and sourced directly from verified local farmers in Nepal. We maintain complete transparency in our supply chain and can provide certification documents upon request. Our farms follow strict organic farming practices without the use of synthetic pesticides, fertilizers, or GMOs.

We currently deliver across the Kathmandu Valley including Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur. We also offer delivery to major cities like Pokhara, Chitwan, and Biratnagar with slightly longer delivery times. For areas outside our regular delivery zones, please contact us to discuss shipping options.

Our vegetables and fruits are harvested fresh daily and delivered within 24-48 hours. We work directly with farmers to ensure minimal time between harvest and delivery. For dairy products, we maintain a cold chain to preserve freshness. All products display their harvest/production date so you know exactly how fresh they are.

We stand behind the quality of our products. If you're not satisfied with any item, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photos of the product. We offer full refunds or replacements for damaged, spoiled, or incorrect items. For perishable goods, we recommend inspecting your order immediately upon delivery.

Yes! We offer weekly and bi-weekly subscription boxes for regular customers. Choose from our curated vegetable boxes, fruit baskets, or create your own custom subscription. Subscribers enjoy 10% discount on all orders and priority delivery. You can pause, modify, or cancel your subscription anytime.

We're always looking to partner with organic farmers across Nepal! If you practice organic farming and are interested in joining our network, please reach out through our contact form or email us at farmers@organicshopnepal.com. We'll schedule a visit to your farm and discuss partnership opportunities.

We accept multiple payment options for your convenience: Cash on Delivery (COD), eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay, bank transfers, and all major debit/credit cards. For subscription orders, we recommend setting up automatic payments through eSewa or Khalti for a seamless experience.
