Growing up in the hills of Nuwakot, I watched my mother care for our family's cows with such dedication. She used to say that happy cows give the sweetest milk. I didn't understand what she meant until I started my own dairy farm.

After my husband passed away, I needed a way to support my three children. With just two cows and a small piece of land, I started what would become my life's work – producing the purest, most natural dairy products in the region.

> "My cows are not just animals – they're family. When you treat them with love, they give you back the purest milk."

## The Organic Way

When I started, organic farming wasn't a trend – it was simply how my mother and grandmother had always done things. We fed our cows with grass from our own pastures, supplemented with locally grown grains. No hormones, no antibiotics unless absolutely necessary for the animal's health.

The milk our cows produce has a richness you can't find in commercial dairy products. The ghee has that golden color and nutty aroma that comes only from grass-fed cows living happy lives in the mountains.

## Challenges Along the Way

Running a dairy farm as a woman in rural Nepal comes with its challenges:

- **Physical Demands** - Milking cows, cleaning sheds, and managing the farm requires strength and endurance
- **Market Access** - Getting our products to consumers in Kathmandu was difficult before partnering with Organic Shop Nepal
- **Financial Pressure** - Organic farming doesn't provide quick returns; patience is essential
- **Education** - Many people don't understand the value of organic dairy products

## Joining Organic Shop Nepal

Two years ago, a team from Organic Shop Nepal visited our village looking for authentic organic producers. They were impressed by our farming practices and the quality of our dairy products.

Since then, they've helped us:

1. Improve our storage and transportation of dairy products
2. Get proper organic certification
3. Connect with customers who value pure, chemical-free dairy
4. Receive training on maintaining the highest quality standards

## Our Products

Today, our small farm produces:

- **Fresh Milk** - Delivered daily to Kathmandu customers
- **Organic Ghee** - Made using traditional methods, clarified over slow fire
- **Farm Butter** - Churned fresh every morning
- **Paneer** - Soft and fresh, made from our morning milk
- **Yogurt** - Thick, creamy, and naturally set

## A Message to Other Women Farmers

To the women who are thinking about starting their own farm or dairy business – don't let anyone tell you that you can't do it. Yes, it's hard work. Yes, there will be days when you feel like giving up. But there is nothing more rewarding than building something with your own hands that feeds your family and community.

The hills of Nepal are full of opportunities for those willing to work hard and stay true to traditional, organic practices.
